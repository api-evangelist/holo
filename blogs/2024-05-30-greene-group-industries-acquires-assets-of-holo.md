OCEANSIDE, Calif., March 28, 2024 -- Greene Group Industries, Inc., ("GGI"), a leader in the development and manufacture of complex components via metal injection molding, has announced the acquisition of the assets of Holo, Inc. Holo's patented PureForm™ additive manufacturing technology enables rapid prototyping and scaled production of complex metal parts.
